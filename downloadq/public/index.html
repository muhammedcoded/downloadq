GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently asked questions

Can DownloadQ save YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram posts?

DownloadQ attempts public YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X and LinkedIn post links via its server extractor. Some sites block servers, require sign-in, or restrict downloads, so support varies by video.

Do I need an account?

No. Supported public links work without creating an account.

Are my uploaded files sent to a server?

Video links are processed by our server. We do not require account registration.

Which formats are supported?

Supported social videos can be downloaded as MP4 or extracted as MP3 when available. Public direct media links retain their original format.

Can I use this for someone else's content?

Only save media that you own, have permission to download, or are otherwise authorized to use. Respect copyrights, privacy and platform terms.
